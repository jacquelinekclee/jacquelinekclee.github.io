We leveraged Transcriptome-Wide Association Studies to identify genes that are associated with irritable bowel disease using various genetic data, bioinformatics tools, and simple linear regression. In the end, we identified 7 genes that are highly associated with IBD. Such techniques have implications for preventative healthcare, in which they could be employed to help diagnose individuals before onset of symptoms/disease.
