project-title: 🎵⏪ Play Back - Music Streaming History Deep Dive
repo: https://github.com/jacquelinekclee/lastfm_analysis
photo: /assets/project-files/playback.png
description: Play Back enables last.fm users from any music streaming service to unlock personalized insights and predictions from their music listening history. I wanted to go a step further than solutions like Spotify Wrapped and Tidal Rewind (my personal music streaming app of choice) and provide a way for music and data lovers like myself to dive deeper into what music they listen to. I leveraged Claude as a sounding board and assistant to provide skeleton code and when building this app.
highlights:
    - Built app for end-to-end experience of analyzing music listening history with capabilities for allowing users to upload their raw data, customize views for their analysis, configure their own clustering model, and download their processed data and ML model output. 
    - 3 main components of app spanning data analysis, data visualization, and machine learning - (1) streaming history overview dashboard, (2) interactive calendar heatmap of streaming history, (4) k-means clustering model for grouping user listening sessions to uncover user streaming tendencies. 
skills:
    - Data cleaning and processing 
    - Data visualization
    - Machine learning - hyperparameter tuning, model training, and interpretation of output
    - Web app building
technologies: 
    - Streamlit
    - Python
    - scikit-learn
    - Claude (for Gen AI-assisted ideation and building)
